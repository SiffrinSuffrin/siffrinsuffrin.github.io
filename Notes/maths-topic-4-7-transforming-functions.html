Maths Topic 4.7 - Transforming Functions

Known

You can apply transformations to unknown functions by considering how specific features are transformed.
The rules used are in Maths Topic 4.5 - Translating Graphs and Maths Topic 4.6 - Stretching Graphs, with function notation from Maths Topic 2.3 - Functions. The features to track include the roots and turning points of Maths Topic 2.4 - Quadratic Graphs, Maths Topic 4.1 - Cubic Graphs and Maths Topic 4.2 - Quartic Graphs, and the asymptotes of Maths Topic 4.3 - Reciprocal Graphs.
