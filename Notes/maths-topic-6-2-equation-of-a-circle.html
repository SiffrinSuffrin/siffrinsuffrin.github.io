Maths Topic 6.2 - Equation Of A Circle

Center

The equation of a circle with center \((a,b)\) and radius \(r\) is \((x-a)^2 + (y-b)^2=r^2\)

Alternative Equation

\(x^2+y^2+2fx+2gy+c=0\) where the center is \((-f,-g)\), and radius is \(\sqrt{f^2+g^2-c}\). Just complete the square brotato.
The equation is the distance formula from Maths Topic 5.4 - Length and Area with a fixed distance r. See Maths Topic 6.3 - Intersections Of Straight Lines And Circles for intersections with lines, Maths Topic 6.4 - Use Tangent And Chord Properties for tangents and chords and Maths Topic 6.5 - Circles And Triangles for circles and triangles. A circle with its centre moved is a translation, see Maths Topic 4.5 - Translating Graphs.
