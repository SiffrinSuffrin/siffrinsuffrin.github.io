Maths Topic 9.3 - Areas Of Triangles

2 Sides And 1 Angle

\(Area=\frac{1}{2}ab\sin{C}\)
The sides and angles needed are found with Maths Topic 9.1 - The Cosine Rule and Maths Topic 9.2 - The Sine Rule.
