Physics Topic 4.1.1 - Electric Circuit Components

Introduction

Electricity is a large and diverse field, with many concepts.
Circuits require:

  • Energy source
  • Closed path/complete circuit
  • Electrical components

Example Electrical Circuit Components

Component Purpose Change in resistance
Switch Turn a circuit on and off Low when closed, very high when open (due to the gap)
Cell/Battery (2+ cells) Provides energy Negligible
Terminals Ditto Low
Lamp Energy to light Will increase with current
Fixed resistor Controls the amount of current moving through a component Depends on the resistor

Conventional Current Vs Electron Flow

Conventional current flows from the positive terminal to the negative one. Electrons physically move from the negative terminal to the positive.
