Maths Topic 12.5 - Differentiating Functions With Two Or More Terms

One At A Time

If \(y=f(x)+/-g(x)\), \(\frac{dy}{dx}=f'(x)+/-g'(x)\)
Each term uses Maths Topic 12.3 - Differentiating X^N or Maths Topic 12.4 - Differentiating Quadratics. Brackets may need Maths Topic 1.2 - Expanding Brackets first, fractions may need Maths Topic 7.1 - Algebraic Fractions or Maths Topic 7.2 - Dividing Polynomials to split them into terms, and roots need Maths Topic 1.4 - Negative And Fractional Indices. Cubics and quartics are in Maths Topic 4.1 - Cubic Graphs and Maths Topic 4.2 - Quartic Graphs.
