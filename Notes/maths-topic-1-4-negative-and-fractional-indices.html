Maths Topic 1.4 - Negative And Fractional Indices

Further Indices

While a few index laws were given in Maths Topic 1.1 - Index Laws, there are more! These allow you to account for any rational powers in the index.

  • \(a^{\frac{1}{m}} = \sqrt[m]{a}\)
  • \(a^{\frac{n}{m}} = \sqrt[m]{a^n}\)
  • \(a^{-m} = \frac{1}{a^m}\)
  • \(a^0 = 1\)
    Negative indices appear in derived SI units such as s^-2 (Physics Topic 2.1.1 - Physical Quantities And Units) and in the exponent in Computer Science Topic 31 - Floating Point Arithmetic. Fractional indices are another way of writing the roots in Maths Topic 1.5 - Surds. Roots and reciprocals have to be rewritten as powers like these before they can be differentiated, see Maths Topic 12.3 - Differentiating X^N.
