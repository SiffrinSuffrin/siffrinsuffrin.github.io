Physics Topic 3.1.1 - Definitions In Kinematics

Introduction

  • Speed = Distance per unit of time
  • Displacement = Distance moved from a given refrence point
  • Velocity = Displacement per unit of time
  • Acceleration = Change in velocity per unit of time
    Distance and speed are scalars, while displacement and velocity are vectors (Physics Topic 2.2.1 - Scalar And Vector Quantities).

Speed And Velocity

\(speed = \frac{distance}{time}\)
\(velocity = \frac{displacement}{time}\)

Interpreting Displacement-Time Graphs

Total distance is distance outwards + distance returned
Total displacement is distance outwards - distance returned
See Physics Topic 3.1.2 - Graphs Of Motion for more on interpreting graphs of motion, and Physics Topic 3.1.3 - Constant Acceleration Equations for the equations that link these quantities.
Distance, speed and time are used for thinking distance in Physics Topic 3.1.6 - Car Stopping Distances. Acceleration is linked to force in Physics Topic 3.2.1 - Force And The Newton and Physics Topic 3.2.2 - Dynamics. Velocity and acceleration are rates of change, which is what differentiation finds (Maths Topic 12.11 - Modelling With Differentiation), and acceleration is the second derivative of displacement (Maths Topic 12.8 - Second Order Derivatives).
