Maths Topic 6.1 - Midpoints And Perpendicular Bisectors

Finding The Midpoint

The midpoint of a line segment can be found by averaging the endpoints.
Take \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\). The midpoint is \((\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\frac{y_1+y_2}{2})\)

Perpendicular Bisectors

The perpendicular bisector of a line is one that passes through the midpoint of a line, and its gradient is the negative reciprocal.
Gradients come from Maths Topic 5.1 - Y = MX + C. The equation of the bisector is found with Maths Topic 5.2 - Equations Of Straight Lines, and the distance between the endpoints is in Maths Topic 5.4 - Length and Area. Perpendicular bisectors are used for chords in Maths Topic 6.4 - Use Tangent And Chord Properties and for circumcentres in Maths Topic 6.5 - Circles And Triangles.
