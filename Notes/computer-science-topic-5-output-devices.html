Computer Science Topic 5 - Output Devices

LCD Monitors

Liquid crystal display monitors contain groups of RGB liquid crystal cells (behind colour filters) to form each pixel. They are backlit with LEDs. They have many benefits, such as:

  • Reach max brightness immediately
  • Sharp image
  • Produce a bright light
  • Thinner
  • Last longer
  • Consume little power

OLED Monitors

Brighter, thinner, and lighter (they're also harder, better, faster, stronger, as was famously sung about) than an LCD monitor. They are made of plastic instead of glass, need no backlights and refresh faster. The LEDs used in backlights and OLED screens are diodes that only conduct one way, see Physics Topic 4.2.3 - Resistance Of Circuit Components.

Laser Printers

Laser fired at print drum, charging to attract toner. Toner then deposited onto paper.

Inkjet Printer

Sprays tiny dots of ink onto a piece of paper. Slower, but more accurate than a laser printer.

Dot Matrix Printer

Matrix of pins strike the paper to embed the text onto the paper. Slow, noisy and bad quality, but practically indestructible.

3D Printer

Prints. But with an additional D. Wowie.

Projector

Light from a bright lamp is projected through a small screen, then a lens, onto a wall or other surface in order to display an image, similar to a monitor. See Computer Science Topic 3 - Types Of Processor for the GPU, which produces the image sent to a monitor or projector.

Computer Speakers

Produces sound, allowing the playing of music and other audio use. High quality speakers can be bought separately, but most have built in ones.

Actuators

Computer controlled motors, used in conjunction with sensors to control a mechanism. See Computer Science Topic 4 - Input Devices for sensors.
Peripheral management in Computer Science Topic 7 - Functions Of An Operating System covers how the operating system talks to output devices, and Computer Science Topic 24 - HTML And CSS covers the web pages a browser displays on them. Embedded operating systems drive control device outputs like actuators, and each output device needs a device driver, see Computer Science Topic 8 - Types Of Operating System.
