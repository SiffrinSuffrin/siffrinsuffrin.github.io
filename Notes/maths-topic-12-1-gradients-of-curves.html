Maths Topic 12.1 - Gradients Of Curves

Intro

The gradient of a curve is constantly changing. A tangent can be used to find the instantaneous gradient, the gradient of the tangent of the curve at the given point.
The gradient of a straight line is constant, see Maths Topic 5.1 - Y = MX + C, but a curve needs a tangent at each point. A tangent to a circle is a special case, see Maths Topic 6.4 - Use Tangent And Chord Properties. The gradient of a displacement-time graph is the velocity, see Physics Topic 3.1.2 - Graphs Of Motion.
