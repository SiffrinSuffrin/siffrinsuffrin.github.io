Maths Topic 12.2 - Finding The Derivative

Points

As two points on the line f(x) (A, and B), move closer to each other, the gradient of the chord AB becomes closer to the gradient of the tangent at A.
This can be formalised, \(A = (x_0,f(x_0))\), \(B = (x_0+h, f(x_0+h))\).
The gradient is change in Y over change in X, so the gradient is \(\frac{f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)}{h}\).
Therefore, the derivative, or gradient (\(f'(x)\)/\(\frac{dy}{dx}\)) is equal to \(\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)}{h}\). This is differentiation from first principle.
The gradient of a chord comes from Maths Topic 5.1 - Y = MX + C, and the idea of a tangent is in Maths Topic 12.1 - Gradients Of Curves. The algebra needs Maths Topic 1.2 - Expanding Brackets, and bigger powers are faster with Maths Topic 8.3 - The Binomial Expansion. The shortcut that replaces this method is Maths Topic 12.3 - Differentiating X^N.
