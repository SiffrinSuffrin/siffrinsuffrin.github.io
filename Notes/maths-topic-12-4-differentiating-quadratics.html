Maths Topic 12.4 - Differentiating Quadratics

Curve

For \(y=ax^2+bx+C\), \(\frac{dy}{dx}=2ax+b\), constant terms disappear and single x terms become constant terms.
This is a special case of Maths Topic 12.3 - Differentiating X^N. The graph is in Maths Topic 2.4 - Quadratic Graphs, and the turning point there (also found by Maths Topic 2.2 - Completing The Square) is where the gradient is zero, see Maths Topic 12.9 - Stationary Points. Longer expressions are in Maths Topic 12.5 - Differentiating Functions With Two Or More Terms.
