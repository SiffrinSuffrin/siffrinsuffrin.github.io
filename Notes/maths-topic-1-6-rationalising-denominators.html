Maths Topic 1.6 - Rationalising Denominators

Irrational Denominators

An irrational denominator is any denominator with a surd in it.

Rationalisation Rules

  • \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{a}}\), multiply top and bottom by \(\sqrt{a}\)
  • \(\frac{1}{a + \sqrt{b}}\) multiply top and bottom by \(a - \sqrt{b}\) (yes, this is Maths Topic 1.3 - Factorising's difference of two squares)
  • \(\frac{1}{a - \sqrt{b}}\) multiply top and bottom by \(a + \sqrt{b}\)
