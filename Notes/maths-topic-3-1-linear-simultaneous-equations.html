Maths Topic 3.1 - Linear Simultaneous Equations

Solving

Linear simultaneous equations in 2 unknowns have one set of values that make 2 equations true at the same time (intersection point between the two lines). They can be solved by elimination or substitution (putting one in the other)
See Maths Topic 3.3 - Simultaneous Equations On Graphs for the graphical meaning, Maths Topic 3.2 - Quadratic Simultaneous Equations for when one equation is quadratic, and Maths Topic 5.1 - Y = MX + C for the equation of a line. Lines given by a point and a gradient are written out with Maths Topic 5.2 - Equations Of Straight Lines first, and solving two perpendicular bisectors simultaneously finds the centre of a circle in Maths Topic 6.5 - Circles And Triangles.
