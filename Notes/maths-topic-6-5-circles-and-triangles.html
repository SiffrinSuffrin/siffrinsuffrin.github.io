Maths Topic 6.5 - Circles And Triangles

Interaction

A triangle consists of 3 vertices. It is always possible to draw a circle through the three vertices of any given triangle. This is called the circumcircle of the triangle, with the center being the circumcenter (where the perpendicular bisectors of each side intersect)
If PQR angle = 90, the diameter is PR
The angle in a semicircle is always a right angle

Center

Find the equations of the bisectors, and then get the point of intersection
The radius is the distance from the circumcentre to a vertex (Maths Topic 5.4 - Length and Area), and the equation of the circle is in Maths Topic 6.2 - Equation Of A Circle. Triangles without a right angle are solved with Maths Topic 9.1 - The Cosine Rule and Maths Topic 9.2 - The Sine Rule, and their area is in Maths Topic 9.3 - Areas Of Triangles.
