Maths Topic 7.2 - Dividing Polynomials

Definition

A polynomial is a finite expression with non-negative whole number indices

Division

You can use long division to divide a polynomial with \((x+/-p)\) where P is any constant
A first factor can be found with Maths Topic 7.3 - The Factor Theorem, then used to divide. Dividing terms uses the index laws in Maths Topic 1.1 - Index Laws, and the result can be used in Maths Topic 1.3 - Factorising and Maths Topic 7.1 - Algebraic Fractions. Cubics and quartics are polynomials, see Maths Topic 4.1 - Cubic Graphs and Maths Topic 4.2 - Quartic Graphs. Negative and fractional indices (Maths Topic 1.4 - Negative And Fractional Indices) are not allowed in a polynomial.
