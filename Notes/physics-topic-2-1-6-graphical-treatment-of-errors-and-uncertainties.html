Physics Topic 2.1.6 - Graphical Treatment Of Errors and Uncertainties

Error Bars

For every point, draw a line above and below the point, for where your uncertainties lie (see Physics Topic 2.1.5 - Absolute And Percentage Uncertainties).

Using Graphs

To make your graph as effective as possible:

  • Scale the graph, so the plotted points cover as much of the graph as possible
  • Clear plotting
  • Clear line of best/worst fit (see Maths Topic 5.5 - Modelling With Straight Lines)
  • Gradient can be calculated easily using two points on the line that are far apart but within range (see Maths Topic 5.1 - Y = MX + C)
  • Y-Intercept should be read clearly
    The gradient and intercept often carry physical meaning, such as velocity on a displacement-time graph (Physics Topic 3.1.2 - Graphs Of Motion).

Determining Uncertainty In The Gradient

  • Add error bars
  • Draw line of best fit, discard major outliers
  • Calculate gradient of best fit line
  • Do the same for the worst fit line (worst acceptable line, still passing through all the error bars. This will be either the steepest possible line of fit, or the least steep line of fit (ie try drawing from bottom of lowest error bar to top of highest error bar and vice versa.))
  • Work out the absolute uncertainty between the two (take positive value) via \((gradient Of Best Fit Line) − (gradient Of Worst Fit Line)\)
  • (absolute uncertainty can also be found through \(\frac{worstSteep-worstShallow}{2}\))
  • Calculate % uncertainty via \((\frac{uncertainty}{gradient Of Best Fit Line})×100\)

Determining Uncertainty In The Y-Intercept

  • Extrapolate line of best fit for the best y-intercept value
  • Draw min/max gradient, extrapolate to y-intercept
  • Determine difference between min and max y-intercepts, halve
  • \((\frac{uncertainty}{bestYInterceptValue})×100\)
    Worked uncertainty with voltage and current readings is in Physics Topic 1 - Physics Practical Endorsement, and Ohm's law, the usual straight line graph in electricity, is in Physics Topic 4.2.2 - Resistance And Ohm's Law.
