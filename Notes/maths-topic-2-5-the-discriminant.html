Maths Topic 2.5 - The Discriminant

Finding Roots

Where \(f(x)=ax^2+bx+c\):

  • if \(b^2-4ac>0\): 2 roots
  • if \(b^2-4ac=0\): 1 root
  • if \(b^2-4ac<0\): 0 real roots
    See Maths Topic 2.1 - Solving Quadratic Equations for the quadratic formula this comes from, Maths Topic 2.4 - Quadratic Graphs for what it means on a graph, and Maths Topic 3.2 - Quadratic Simultaneous Equations for its use with simultaneous equations.
    The discriminant also counts the intersections of a line and a circle, see Maths Topic 6.3 - Intersections Of Straight Lines And Circles. Splitting a problem into cases like these is proof by exhaustion, see Maths Topic 7.5 - Methods Of Proof.
