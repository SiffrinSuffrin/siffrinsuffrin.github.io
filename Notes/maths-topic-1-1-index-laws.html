Maths Topic 1.1 - Index Laws

Laws Of Indices

When you are doing maths operations with indices, there are a few rules to follow.

  • \(a ^ m × a ^ n = a ^ {m + n}\)
  • \(a ^ m ÷ a ^ n = a ^ {m - n}\)
  • \((a ^ m) ^ n = a ^ {mn}\)
  • \((ab) ^ n = a ^ n b ^ n\)
    See Maths Topic 1.4 - Negative And Fractional Indices for negative and fractional indices. Indices are used for the prefixes in Physics Topic 2.1.1 - Physical Quantities And Units, for powers of 10 in Physics Topic 2.1.2 - Estimating Physical Quantities, for binary prefixes in Computer Science Topic 29 - ASCII and Unicode, and for the exponent in Computer Science Topic 31 - Floating Point Arithmetic.
    They are also used to simplify powers in Maths Topic 7.1 - Algebraic Fractions and Maths Topic 7.2 - Dividing Polynomials, for the place values in Computer Science Topic 28 - Primitive Data Types, Binary and Hexadecimal, for shifts in Computer Science Topic 32 - Bitwise Manipulation And Masks, and for address bus widths in Computer Science Topic 2 - Processor Performance.
