Physics Topic 3.1.2 - Graphs Of Motion

Displacement Time Graphs

Shows the displacement of a moving object vs time. Displacement on Y and time on X. Gradient = instantaneous velocity.

Interpreting Distance Time Graphs

  • Straight diagonal = constant velocity
  • Flat line = zero velocity
  • Curve of increasing gradient = increasing velocity
  • Curve of decreasing gradient = decreasing velocity

Interpreting Velocity Time Graphs

  • Straight diagonal = constant acceleration/deceleration
  • Curve = non-uniform change in velocity
    Area below is the total displacement.
    Under constant acceleration a displacement-time graph is curved, because \(s=ut+\frac{1}{2}at^2\) is a quadratic in t, see Maths Topic 2.4 - Quadratic Graphs. On a curved displacement-time graph the instantaneous velocity is the gradient of the tangent at that point, and Maths Topic 12.2 - Finding The Derivative shows how the gradient of a chord gets closer to the tangent as the two points move together. A velocity-time curve that levels off is an object reaching terminal velocity, see Physics Topic 3.2.3 - Drag And Terminal Velocity.
