Maths Topic 1.2 - Expanding Brackets

Multiplication

To find the product of two expressions multiply each term in one by the other
\((x+5)(4x-2y+3) = x(4x-2y+3) + 5(4x-2y+3)\)
\(=4x^2-2xy+4x+20x-10y+15\)
\(=4x^2-2xy+23x-10y+15\)
The reverse process is Maths Topic 1.3 - Factorising. Expanding brackets is also used to multiply binary numbers by splitting one into powers of two, see Computer Science Topic 32 - Bitwise Manipulation And Masks. Expanding is also used when rationalising denominators (Maths Topic 1.6 - Rationalising Denominators) and for proving identities (Maths Topic 7.4 - Mathematical Proof), and a bracket raised to a power has a faster method in Maths Topic 8.3 - The Binomial Expansion.
