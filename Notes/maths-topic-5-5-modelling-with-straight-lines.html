Maths Topic 5.5 - Modelling With Straight Lines

Proportionality

Two quantities are in propotion when they increase at the same rate. The graph of these is a straight line through the origin.

Linear Model

A linear model can be used to show the relationship between to variables, X and Y. The graph of a linear model is a straight line, and are related by \(y=mx+c\). A linear model doesnt have to be exactly on the line, as long as all the points are close.
See Maths Topic 5.1 - Y = MX + C for the equation of a line. Lines of best fit are used in Physics Topic 2.1.6 - Graphical Treatment Of Errors and Uncertainties, and Physics Topic 3.1.6 - Car Stopping Distances has an example of proportionality, since thinking distance is proportional to speed. Quadratic models are in Maths Topic 2.6 - Modelling With Quadratics, and a straight line through the origin is also the current-voltage graph of a resistor in Physics Topic 4.2.3 - Resistance Of Circuit Components. A linear model has a constant rate of change, and models where the rate of change varies use Maths Topic 12.11 - Modelling With Differentiation.
