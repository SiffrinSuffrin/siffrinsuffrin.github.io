Maths Topic 12.11 - Modelling With Differentiation

Models

\(\frac{dy}{dx}\) is just any rate of change. If you replace x and y with two variables that represent real quantities, you can use the derivative to model lots of real-life situations involving rates of change.
Maximum and minimum values come from Maths Topic 12.9 - Stationary Points, and whether a quantity is growing or shrinking from Maths Topic 12.7 - Increasing And Decreasing Functions. Compare the models in Maths Topic 2.6 - Modelling With Quadratics and Maths Topic 5.5 - Modelling With Straight Lines, where the rate of change had to be found without calculus. Velocity and acceleration are rates of change, see Physics Topic 3.1.1 - Definitions In Kinematics.
