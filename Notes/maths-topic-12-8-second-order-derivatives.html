Maths Topic 12.8 - Second Order Derivatives

Twice Over

Differentiate a function twice to get the derivative of the derivative, the gradient of the gradient. \(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}\)
The second derivative classifies stationary points, see Maths Topic 12.9 - Stationary Points, and is the gradient of the graph in Maths Topic 12.10 - Sketching Gradient Functions. Acceleration is the second derivative of displacement, see Physics Topic 3.1.1 - Definitions In Kinematics and Physics Topic 3.1.2 - Graphs Of Motion. It uses Maths Topic 12.3 - Differentiating X^N twice.
