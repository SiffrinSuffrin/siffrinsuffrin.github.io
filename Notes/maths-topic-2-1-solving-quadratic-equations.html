Maths Topic 2.1 - Solving Quadratic Equations

Factorising

Write equation in the form \(ax^2+bx+c=0\), factorise left hand side, set each factor equal to zero and solve to find the values of x

Quadratic Formula

The solutions to \(ax^2+bx+c=0\) are given by the formula \(\frac{-b±\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}\)
The part under the square root, \(b^2-4ac\), is the Maths Topic 2.5 - The Discriminant. The formula can be derived by Maths Topic 2.2 - Completing The Square. See Physics Topic 3.1.4 - Free Fall And Projectile Motion for a physics use, finding the time of flight of a projectile. When \(b^2-4ac\) is not a square number the answers are left in surd form, see Maths Topic 1.5 - Surds. Solving a quadratic is also the main step in Maths Topic 3.2 - Quadratic Simultaneous Equations and Maths Topic 3.5 - Quadratic Inequalities.
