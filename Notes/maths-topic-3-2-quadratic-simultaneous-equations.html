Maths Topic 3.2 - Quadratic Simultaneous Equations

Solving

One equation quadratic, one linear (compare Maths Topic 3.1 - Linear Simultaneous Equations). Substitute linear into quadratic, simplify, then solve using quadratic equation (Maths Topic 2.1 - Solving Quadratic Equations). Up to two solutions, because a straight line can touch a quadratic curve in at most two places

Number of solutions

For any pair of simultaneous equations that produce a quadratic of the form \(ax^2+bx+c=0\):

  • if \(b^2-4ac>0\): 2 solutions
  • if \(b^2-4ac=0\): 1 solution
  • if \(b^2-4ac<0\): 0 solutions
    (see Maths Topic 2.5 - The Discriminant)
    Substituting a straight line into the equation of a circle also gives a quadratic, so the same method works, see Maths Topic 6.3 - Intersections Of Straight Lines And Circles.
