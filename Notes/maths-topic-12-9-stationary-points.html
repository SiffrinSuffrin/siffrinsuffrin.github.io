Maths Topic 12.9 - Stationary Points

Gradient Zero

Any point where \(f'(x)=0\) is a stationary point.
For any small positive h on any stationary point:

Type \(f'(x-h)\) \(f'(x+h)\)
Local Max + -
Local Min - +
Inflection +/- +/-

Double diff can also be used
\(f''(a)>0\), min
\(f''(a)<0\), max
The gradient comes from Maths Topic 12.3 - Differentiating X^N and the sign test from Maths Topic 12.7 - Increasing And Decreasing Functions, with the double differentiation test in Maths Topic 12.8 - Second Order Derivatives. A quadratic's turning point is in Maths Topic 2.4 - Quadratic Graphs, and cubics and quartics have more stationary points, see Maths Topic 4.1 - Cubic Graphs and Maths Topic 4.2 - Quartic Graphs. Finding a maximum or minimum is used in Maths Topic 12.11 - Modelling With Differentiation, and the graph of \(f'(x)\) is in Maths Topic 12.10 - Sketching Gradient Functions.
