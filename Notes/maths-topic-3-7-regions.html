Maths Topic 3.7 - Regions

Introduction

y > f(x) represents all points on the grid above the curve, and vice versa.
y > means dotted line, y >= means solid line
Boundary lines are drawn from equations like those in Maths Topic 5.1 - Y = MX + C. See Maths Topic 3.6 - Inequalities On Graphs for inequalities on graphs and Maths Topic 3.4 - Linear Inequalities for linear inequalities. The curve f(x) can be a quadratic (Maths Topic 2.4 - Quadratic Graphs), and the algebra for that case is in Maths Topic 3.5 - Quadratic Inequalities.
