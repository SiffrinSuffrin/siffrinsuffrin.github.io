Maths Topic 5.2 - Equations Of Straight Lines

Defining A Straight Line

Straight lines can be defined with either one point on the line and a gradient, or two different points on the line.
The equation of a line with gradient m that passses through the point \((x_1, y_1)\) can be written as \(y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)\)
See Maths Topic 5.1 - Y = MX + C for y = mx + c, Maths Topic 5.3 - Parallel And Perpendicular Lines for parallel and perpendicular lines, and Maths Topic 3.1 - Linear Simultaneous Equations for finding where two lines meet. The same \(y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)\) form gives the equation of a perpendicular bisector in Maths Topic 6.1 - Midpoints And Perpendicular Bisectors, a tangent to a circle in Maths Topic 6.4 - Use Tangent And Chord Properties, and a tangent or normal to a curve in Maths Topic 12.6 - Gradients, Tangents, And Normals.
