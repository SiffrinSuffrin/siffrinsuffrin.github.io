Maths Topic 7.1 - Algebraic Fractions

Division

You can simplify an algebraic fraction by factorising the numerator and denominator, then cancelling common factors.
Factorising is covered in Maths Topic 1.3 - Factorising, cancelling powers uses Maths Topic 1.1 - Index Laws, and a fraction that does not factorise easily can be tackled with Maths Topic 7.3 - The Factor Theorem and Maths Topic 7.2 - Dividing Polynomials.
