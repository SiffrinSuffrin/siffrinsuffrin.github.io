Maths Topic 8.3 - The Binomial Expansion

Expanding Brackets... Faster.

\((a+b)^n = a^n + ^nC_1 a^{n-1}b + ^nC_2 a^{n-2}b^2 + ... + ^nC_r a^{n-r}b^r + ... + b^n\)
This is the faster version of Maths Topic 1.2 - Expanding Brackets for a bracket raised to a power. The coefficients come from Maths Topic 8.1 - Pascal's Triangle for small n, or from \(^nC_r\) in Maths Topic 8.2 - Factorial Notation for any n. See Maths Topic 8.4 - Solving Binomial Problems for finding a single term and Maths Topic 8.5 - Binomial Estimation for using the first few terms as an approximation. Expanding \((x+h)^n\) this way is needed when differentiating from first principles, see Maths Topic 12.2 - Finding The Derivative.
