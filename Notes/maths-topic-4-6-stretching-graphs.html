Maths Topic 4.6 - Stretching Graphs

X and Y

The graph of \(y=af(x)\) is a stretch in the vertical by a
The graph of \(y=f(ax)\) is a stretch in the horizontal by \(\frac{1}{a}\)

Reflection

The graph of \(y=-f(x)\) is a reflection in the x axis
The graph of \(y=f(-x)\) is a reflection in the y axis
See Maths Topic 4.5 - Translating Graphs for translations and Maths Topic 4.7 - Transforming Functions for applying these to an unknown function, and Maths Topic 2.3 - Functions for function notation. Stretching \(y=\frac{1}{x}\) gives the graphs in Maths Topic 4.3 - Reciprocal Graphs.
