Physics Topic 4.2.4 - Resistivity

Introduction

Resistance depends on a number of factors, such as length, cross sectional area, or temperature. Resistivity on the other hand is not affected by changing the physical dimensions of a material, and is a fixed value per piece of material. Where p is resistivity, R is resistance, A is area, and l is length, \(p = \frac{RA}{l}\).

Using Resistivity

Resistivity can be used to find voids under buildings and roads, if the value is different than expected.
The diameter of a wire, needed for its cross sectional area, is measured with a micrometer (see Physics Topic 1 - Physics Practical Endorsement), with the uncertainty in Physics Topic 2.1.5 - Absolute And Percentage Uncertainties. The effect of the material is linked to metals, semiconductors and insulators in Physics Topic 4.1.3 - Electron Drift Velocity, and the behaviour of different components is in Physics Topic 4.2.3 - Resistance Of Circuit Components.
