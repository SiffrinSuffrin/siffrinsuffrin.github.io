Maths Topic 2.2 - Completing The Square

Rewriting Quadratics

It is frequently useful to rewrite quadratic equations to complete the square: \(ax^2+bx+c=a(x+\frac{b}{2a})^2+(c-\frac{b^2}{4a})\)
See Maths Topic 2.4 - Quadratic Graphs for using this to find a turning point.
The same method gives the centre and radius of a circle from its expanded equation, see Maths Topic 6.2 - Equation Of A Circle, and the turning point it finds is a translation of the basic parabola, see Maths Topic 4.5 - Translating Graphs. The same turning point can be found by differentiating and setting the gradient to zero, see Maths Topic 12.4 - Differentiating Quadratics and Maths Topic 12.9 - Stationary Points. Deriving the quadratic formula this way is an example of proof by deduction, see Maths Topic 7.4 - Mathematical Proof.
