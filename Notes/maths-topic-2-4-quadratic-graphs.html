Maths Topic 2.4 - Quadratic Graphs

Curved Graphs

When \(f(x)=ax^2+bx+c\), \(y=f(x)\) forms a distinctive curved shape called a parabola. If a is positive, the graph will be u shaped. If a is negative, the graph will be n shaped.

Turning point

You may find the point at which the paraboala changes shape by either finding the two roots of the function, and getting the midpoint of the two x values, then using the function to find y, or completing the square, where \(a(x+p)^2+q\) gives you the turning point \((-p,q)\)
Whether there are any roots depends on the Maths Topic 2.5 - The Discriminant, and cubic graphs continue in Maths Topic 4.1 - Cubic Graphs.
A quadratic graph is a transformation of \(y=x^2\), see Maths Topic 4.5 - Translating Graphs and Maths Topic 4.6 - Stretching Graphs. A quadratic model built from this graph is in Maths Topic 2.6 - Modelling With Quadratics, and the region above or below a parabola is in Maths Topic 3.7 - Regions. Under constant acceleration a displacement-time graph is a parabola, see Physics Topic 3.1.2 - Graphs Of Motion.
