Computer Science Topic 54 - Selection

Program Constructs

There are three basic programming constructs: sequence, selection, and iteration (see Computer Science Topic 55 - Iteration).

Sequence

Sequence is two or more statements executed one after another

Selection

Selection statements are used to select which statement will be executed next, depending on some condition. Conditions are formed with relational operators (>, <, ==).
Selection takes the form of if ... then ... else statements (if, elif, else in python), or switch/case statements
Conditions evaluate to Boolean values (Computer Science Topic 28 - Primitive Data Types, Binary and Hexadecimal), and the relational operators work like the inequalities in Maths Topic 3.4 - Linear Inequalities.
Conditions can be combined with AND, OR and NOT, see Computer Science Module 8 Index. The operators and their truth tables are in Computer Science Topic 34 - Boolean Algebra.
