Maths Topic 5.3 - Parallel And Perpendicular Lines

Parallel Lines

Parallel lines are any lines that have the same gradient, but a different y intersect

Perpendicular Lines

Perpendicular lines are at right angles to each other. If a line has a gradient of m, it's perpendicular has a gradient of \(\frac{-1}{m}\) (negative reciprocial). The products of the two gradients will therefore be -1.
See Maths Topic 5.2 - Equations Of Straight Lines for the equation of a line through a point. Perpendicular directions also appear in physics, see Physics Topic 2.2.2 - Scalar And Vector Calculations.
Perpendicular gradients are used to find perpendicular bisectors in Maths Topic 6.1 - Midpoints And Perpendicular Bisectors and tangents to circles in Maths Topic 6.4 - Use Tangent And Chord Properties. The normal to a curve is perpendicular to the tangent, so its gradient is the negative reciprocal too, see Maths Topic 12.6 - Gradients, Tangents, And Normals.
